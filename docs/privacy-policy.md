# Privacy Policy

**Circular Words** · Effective 26 September 2026

Circular Words is a vocabulary game: you read a definition and say the word it
describes. This policy explains what happens to information while you play.
The short version: there are no accounts and no ads, your voice is not
recorded or stored by the app, and nothing is sold.

## Your voice

When a round is running, the app listens through your microphone so it can
check your answer. The listening is done by your device's own speech
recognition service — Google's on Android, Apple's on iOS. Depending on your
device and settings, that service may process the audio on the device or send
it to Google or Apple to be transcribed, under their own privacy policies:

- Google: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- Apple: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>

Circular Words receives only the text the service hears. It uses that text to
check your answer against the current word, and then discards it. The app
never saves recordings or transcripts, and never sends what you said to us or
to any analytics service.

The microphone is used only while a round is on screen. You can refuse the
permission, or turn the microphone off at any time, and play by typing
instead.

## What stays on your device

Your best score for each level, your chosen language and your sound setting
are stored on your device only. Uninstalling the app removes them.

## Usage analytics and crash reports

To learn how the game is played and to fix problems, the app uses two Google
Firebase services:

- **Firebase Analytics** records events such as a level being chosen, a round
  starting and ending, which letters were answered or passed, the score, and
  whether speech recognition was unavailable. Firebase also collects standard
  device information (such as model, operating-system version and language),
  an app-instance identifier, and approximate location derived from your IP
  address.
- **Firebase Crashlytics** records crash reports: the state of the app and the
  device when something went wrong.

These events describe the game, not you. They never contain what you said or
typed. Firebase processes this data on Google's servers under Google's terms:
<https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy>. Analytics data is kept for up to
14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days.

## Sharing your result

When you tap **Share result**, the app makes an image of your score and opens
your device's share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose where it goes.

## What we do not do

- We do not require an account or ask for your name, email or phone number.
- We do not show advertising or use advertising identifiers.
- We do not sell or rent any information.

## Children

Circular Words is intended for learners aged 13 and over, and is not directed
at children under 13.

## Your choices

You can deny or revoke the microphone permission in your device settings;
the game remains fully playable by typing. You can ask us to delete analytics
data associated with your app installation by contacting us at the address
below.
